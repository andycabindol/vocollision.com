What makes Vocollision different

Founded in 2008, Vocollision pushes modern collegiate a cappella with risk-taking arrangements, storytelling through choreography, and a family culture that lifts every member. The group has earned recognition from the International Championships of Collegiate A Cappella (ICCA), including awards for arrangement, choreography, vocal percussion, and championship placements.

Competitive & creative

ICCA-recognized sets that mix classic pop ballads with lesser-known artists and bold sonic choices.

Based in NYC

A New York City group performing across campus, the city, and the collegiate a cappella circuit.

Open to all voice parts

Sopranos, altos, mezzos, tenors, basses, and beatboxers — all majors welcome to audition.
