Audition virtually!

  1. Record a video of yourself singing a verse and a chorus of any song without accompaniment that showcases your vocal talents and range (preferably no musical theater or classical)! If you know how to beatbox, feel free to include a sample of your skills in your video too!

  2. Upload the video to YouTube (it can be unlisted or public)

  3. Submit your audition using the form below

What happens next?

What happens next?
What happens next?

We know auditions can feel intimidating, but we want you to know that we’re so excited to hear you. Vocollision is a family, and we truly WANT you to feel welcome here. 💜

After submitting, if we feel it's a good fit, we'll get back to you within a few days to invite you to an in-person callback. There'll be multiple time options for your in-person callback so don't stress over keeping your schedule open!

In the meantime, stay connected and get to know us better by following @vocollision for updates, behind-the-scenes, and more!

If you have any questions or concerns, email us at nyu.vocollision@gmail.com and we'll get back to you immediately.
