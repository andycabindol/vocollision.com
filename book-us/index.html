Booking FAQ

Can I book an NYU a cappella group for an event in NYC?

Yes. Vocollision is available for booking for campus events, corporate shows, private events, and collaborations in New York City.

How do I book Vocollision?

Submit the form on this page, use Contact, or email nyu.vocollision@gmail.com with your event details.

What types of events does Vocollision perform at?

Collegiate competitions, concerts, campus events, and private or brand bookings when schedules allow.

Is Vocollision available for hire outside NYU?

Yes. Booking inquiries from NYC organizations and events are welcome subject to the group’s availability.
