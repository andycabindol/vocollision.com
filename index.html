Since our founding in 2008, Vocollision has not been afraid to take risks in combining classic pop ballads with songs by lesser-known artists in our arrangements. From Ariana Grande to the beats of Tyler, the Creator, our performances are celebrations of our members and the music we love.

We are an acapella family that challenges the norms of collegiate acapella and singing together.
