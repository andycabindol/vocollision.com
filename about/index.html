Who we are
Who we are

Vocollision is a dynamic group from NYU dedicated to pushing the boundaries of modern acapella. We explore new techniques, genres, styles, and songs.

Founded in 2008 as an Asian-interest acapella group, we’ve since expanded into a community that values diversity and creativity on a wider scale.

We are a family of talented vocalists and musicians, each of us bringing different perspectives and ideas that influence our sound. Together, we craft performances that showcase the power and versatility of the human voice (and captivate audiences and judges!).
