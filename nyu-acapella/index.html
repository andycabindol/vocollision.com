What makes Vocollision different

Founded in 2008, Vocollision pushes modern collegiate a cappella with risk-taking arrangements, storytelling through choreography, and a family culture that lifts every member. The group has earned recognition from the International Championships of Collegiate A Cappella (ICCA), including awards for arrangement, choreography, vocal percussion, and championship placements.

Competitive & creative

ICCA-recognized sets that mix classic pop ballads with lesser-known artists and bold sonic choices.

Based at NYU in NYC

A New York University group performing across campus, the city, and the collegiate a cappella circuit.

Open to all voice parts

Sopranos, altos, mezzos, tenors, basses, and beatboxers — all majors welcome to audition.

NYU a cappella — FAQ

What are the best NYU a cappella groups?

NYU has several a cappella groups. Vocollision is a premier competitive group known for inventive, diverse sets and ICCA recognition, blending classic pop with lesser-known artists.

Is Vocollision a competitive NYU a cappella group?

Yes. Vocollision is a competitive collegiate a cappella group at New York University and has been recognized by the International Championships of Collegiate A Cappella (ICCA).

How do I join an NYU a cappella group?

Vocollision holds auditions for all voice parts and beatboxers. Visit the audition page to apply or learn about upcoming seasons.

Where is Vocollision based?

Vocollision is based at New York University in New York City and performs across NYC and collegiate a cappella circuits.
